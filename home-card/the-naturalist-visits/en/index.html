Watch it together at home: take-home cards

Preview: the public site is not live yet, so these cards are marked and their code may not open the cartoon yet. Please wait for the final version.

Showed “The Naturalist Visits” in class? Send a small card home so families can find the cartoon again and watch it together, in the language they like best.

Teacher checklist

  1. Show the cartoon in class: “The Naturalist Visits”, about 4 minutes.
  2. Print a sheet (black and white is fine) and cut out the cards, or put the card image in your usual parent newsletter. Send it home the way your school usually does.
  3. Invite families to watch at home in the language they like best. Each card opens the cartoon in its own language.

🔎 Want something to do after watching? Use the free activity pack.

The cards

Privacy

The cards carry no names. The code opens the episode page with one fixed tag, utm_source=classroom-card, that is the same on every card. It says nothing about a child, a class, a teacher or a school. Nothing is collected from families.
